# Research — The animal that survived outer space (day 2)

1. Tardigrades survived 10 days of exposed low-Earth orbit in 2007 — vacuum, UV and cosmic radiation — and some laid viable eggs afterward.
   Source: Jönsson et al., Current Biology (2008)

2. They survive by entering the tun state: expelling almost all water and halting metabolism (cryptobiosis).
   Source: Jönsson et al., Current Biology (2008)

3. Tardigrades revived and reproduced after 30.5 years frozen at −20 °C in a museum collection.
   Source: Tsujimoto, Imura & Kanda, Cryobiology (2016)
